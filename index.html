About

Built from drawings, proven on site

I started in 2017 as a Design Engineer, building 3D piping models in AVEVA E3D and running flexibility analysis in CAEPIPE for cryogenic lines carrying LOX, LH₂ and GN₂. In 2019 I moved to Project Engineer and took those designs through fabrication, installation, testing and handover.

Since late 2023 I have been Project In-Charge, with ownership of schedules, manpower, materials, interfaces and quality on process facilities, leading teams of 100 to 125 people without a recorded safety incident.

That path is why I can read a P&ID, check a stress run, release a work front, and sign off a hydrotest pack with equal confidence.

Engineering mindset

A line is one thread, from P&ID to purge.

I see design, fabrication, installation, testing and commissioning as a single chain. A stress run is only good if the spool can be welded and the support can be reached on site. A test pack is only good if the punch list is closed before the gauge is fitted.

Having worked every link of that chain, I look for the problem early, where it costs a drawing revision and not a shutdown.
